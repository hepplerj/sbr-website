A batch of additions landed ahead of the new [Atlas](/atlas/) — new sightlines, a reference glossary, and some infrastructure that the atlas and the essays both build on.

## New sightlines

- **[A Century of Cattle Prices](/sightlines/cattle-prices/)** — annual cattle prices from USDA NASS, deflated to constant dollars with CPI, drawn as a long time series. The economic backdrop to the range politics the rest of the site tracks.
- **[Cosponsorship on Plains Grasslands Bills](/sightlines/grasslands-cosponsorship/)** — a sister network to the existing cosponsorship graph, scoped to grasslands legislation. Same force-directed form, narrower question.

## A sharper region filter

The sightlines region taxonomy was reworked, and the filter mini-map gained an optional **Fenneman (1928) Great Plains** overlay — a physiographic-province boundary that deliberately doesn't follow state lines. It's a first move toward making explicit that "the region" is a constructed analytic unit, not a natural fact: different questions want different boundaries.

## A glossary

A new [Glossary](/glossary/) collects the cattle, range, federal-lands, and movement vocabulary the project leans on — practical, reader-facing definitions oriented to how each term actually shows up in the maps, charts, and prose.

## Charts and infrastructure

- **Swim-lane era labels and period support on line charts**, so the Dust Bowl, Farm Crisis, and Sagebrush-Rebellion spans can be banded on time-series views.
- **Table-of-contents support on section index pages**, not just single essays.
- **Figure infrastructure for narrative essays** — a reusable way to place captioned figures in long-form prose, with the in-progress Great Plains methods essay as the first user.
- A prose pass on the [farm-bankruptcies](/sightlines/) sightline.
