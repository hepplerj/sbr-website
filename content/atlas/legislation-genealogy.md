> **Draft — experiment.** The chronological companion to the
> [similarity matrix](/atlas/legislation-textreuse/) (click any cell of
> which opens the passages a pair shares). Nodes are the eight
> transcribed bills, sized by length and placed in their enactment year;
> an edge is drawn where two bills share at least 90 identical five-word
> phrases, directed from the earlier bill to the later (for same-year
> pairs, from the more fully reproduced text). Enactment years are
> curated (see the build script). Prose is a working draft. Follow the
> [changelog](/updates/).
