---
title: "Exploring historical data"
lede: "Reference instruments: heatmaps, scorecards, and comparative tables for exploring federal land, agriculture, and conservation politics by region and over time."
---

The atlas is a small set of interactive reference views, complementary to the [sightlines](/sightlines/) but used differently. Sightlines pose an argument with a visualization; atlas entries hand you the apparatus to ask your own questions. Hover, click through, sort, drill down.

Atlas entries are pilot-scale right now — one or two Congresses, narrow scopes — and will expand as the methodology proves out.
